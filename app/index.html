      Roadmap to the platinum

      ~200 hours total · difficulty 4/10 · 1 playthrough · all offline. No mission replay and no New Game+, so play clean the first time: never use cheats on your trophy save — cheats disable trophies and saving.

      Stage 1

      The story — 60–80 hours

      Play the five chapters in order, Jason and Lucia. Do the stranger strands as they open (they are time-limited) and meditate at the Chapter 1 spot at Jason’s house.

      20 trophies: the 6 chapter & final-mission trophies, 6 story-mission trophies, 5 stranger finals, Strangers & Freaks, Positive Mental Attitude and Fair Game.

      Stage 2

      100% completion — ~100 hours

      After the final mission the checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion — and story progress already counts. Only five categories need everything: Robberies, Underwater Discoveries, Shooting Ranges, Drone Races, Bret’s Quests.

      Key trophies: A Long Road, The Shootist (gold, all 42 ranges), Top Fun (win at all 14 drone spots).

      The lenient thresholds — you do not need everything

      • Vehicles: 200 of 277
      • Animals: 100 of 255
      • Weapons: 50 of 66
      • Challenges: 70 of 80

      Stage 3

      Cleanup — ~10 hours

      Mop up the remaining collectibles, robberies, activities and miscellaneous trophies — then the platinum “Vice Got a Price!” pops.

      Leftovers: card sets, WAiNK It locations, garage properties, vehicle registrations, shop spending and marked-bill laundering.

      Before you start

      • Meditate at Jason’s house in Chapter 1 — that meditation spot is missable. Keep a separate Chapter 1 save.
      • Start stranger strands early and finish each before pushing far into the story — they close.
      • Never use cheats on your trophy save — they disable trophies and saving.
      • There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so a missed one-off means a new playthrough.
      • Do the 100% work as you go — the checklist only appears after the story, but progress during the story counts.

      Missable trophies

      There is no mission replay and no New Game+ in GTA 6. Eight trophies can be missed or closed off — handle them first, and keep a Chapter 1 save.

      Positive Mental Attitude

      Meditate at every spot across Leonida.

      18 meditation spots in 10 locations, as both Jason and Lucia. The meditation spot at Jason’s house is only available in Chapter 1 — meditate there before Chapter 1 ends, and keep a separate Chapter 1 save as a safety net. Work through the other spots as you explore; each one is a short meditation.
